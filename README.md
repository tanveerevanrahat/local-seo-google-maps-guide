# Local SEO: How to Rank in Google's Map Pack (Top 3)

For a local business, the Map Pack — the top 3 map results — is the most valuable spot on Google. It brings calls, visits, and direction requests before anyone scrolls to organic results. This guide covers what actually moves a business into the top 3.

## What Google looks at

1. **Relevance.** Does your profile match what the user searched? Categories, services, and description must match real services.
2. **Distance.** How close is the business to the searcher? You cannot change this — but you can win the other two factors.
3. **Prominence.** How well-known and trusted is the business? Reviews, photos, citations, and activity all feed this.

## The fundamentals

- **NAP consistency.** Your name, address, and phone number must be identical everywhere — your website, Google Business Profile, directories, and social profiles. Even small differences confuse Google.
- **Right categories.** Pick the primary category that matches your main service, and add relevant secondary categories.
- **Real photos.** Upload real photos of your business, team, and work. Geotagged, descriptively named photos help.
- **Reviews.** Ask happy customers for reviews and reply to every review — good and bad. A steady stream of genuine reviews beats a sudden burst.
- **Posts.** Publish regular updates on your profile: offers, news, tips. It shows Google the business is active.

## The 30-day checklist

- Week 1: Fix NAP everywhere; complete every profile field; upload 10+ real photos
- Week 2: Ask 10 customers for reviews; reply to all existing reviews
- Week 3: Publish 2 profile posts; add all services with descriptions
- Week 4: Build 5 consistent directory citations; publish one local-content post on your site

## Common mistakes

- Keyword-stuffing the business name (against Google's rules)
- Fake reviews — Google removes them and may suspend the profile
- Ignoring reviews, especially negative ones
- Letting NAP drift across directories
- Treating the profile as "set and forget"
